# Scientific Foundations: Generative World Models for Healthcare Digital Twins

BioTwin Omni is built on the paradigm of **Generative World Models for Healthcare**.

## 1. What is a Healthcare World Model?
In modern artificial intelligence, a **World Model** is a computational replica of an environment that allows an agent to simulate future states, test counterfactual actions, and predict outcomes without dangerous real-world trial-and-error.

In human physiology, a Digital Twin World Model operates across multiple physiological scales:
- **Organ Scale:** Cardiovascular hemodynamics (Frank-Starling law, peripheral vascular resistance, Windkessel arterial models).
- **Systemic Scale:** Systemic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (SIRS), neuroendocrine feedback, autoregulation of cerebral and renal blood flow.
- **Molecular Scale:** Pharmacogenomic metabolism rates (CYP2C19, CYP2D6 clearance kinetics).

## 2. Key References from Awesome World Models Repository
This project integrates research principles cataloged in the `research-and-world-models` knowledge base:
- **Cardiovascular Simulation:** Reduced-order 0D/1D hemodynamic models for rapid real-time arterial pressure waveform forecasting.
- **Counterfactual Prediction:** Projecting biomarker trajectories under alternative therapeutic protocols using reinforcement learning and trajectory transformers.
- **Multimodal Clinical Grounding:** Bridging continuous time-series vitals with discrete EHR clinical notes.
